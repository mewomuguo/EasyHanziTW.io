# Traditional Chinese (Taiwan) Reading Challenge

This is a game website for practicing Traditional Chinese characters and Zhuyin as used in Taiwan.

## What is this?

This website helps beginners learn how to read Chinese characters. You can play a game to match Taiwan's characters to their Zhuyin, or match Zhuyin to the right characters.

## Website Features

### 1. Choose Difficulty

The game sorts characters by how often people in Taiwan use them. You can start from Level 1 (the easiest) or mix them all together.

- Level 1 to Level 5 (from basic to advanced)
    
- All Mixed
    

### 2. Choose How to Play

You can choose between two ways to practice:

- **See the character, pick the Zhuyin**: Practice the correct pronunciation used in Taiwan.
    
- **See the Zhuyin, pick the character**: Practice recognizing the correct Taiwan Chinese characters.
    

### 3. Set the Time

You can practice at your own speed:

- No time limit (no pressure)
    
- 1-minute limit
    
- 3-minute limit
    

## After the Game

When the game is over, the website will show you:

- **Your Score**: How many questions you got right and your accuracy.
    
- **Mistakes List**: The words you got wrong, so you can look at them again.
    
- **Slowest Words**: The words you spent the most time thinking about, so you know which Taiwan Chinese characters you need to practice more.
    

## How to Start

1. Open the website link.
    
2. Follow steps 1, 2, and 3 on the screen to choose your settings.
    
3. Click the "Start" button.
    

Website link: [https://mewomuguo.github.io/EasyHanziTW.io/]
# 台灣繁體認字挑戰

這是一個練習台灣繁體中文和注音符號的遊戲網站。

## 這是什麼？

這個網站可以幫助想要學習認字的人，透過遊戲的方式來練習看國字選注音，或是看注音選國字。

## 網站功能

### 1. 挑選難度

遊戲依照字的使用次數分成了不同的等級。你可以從第一級（最簡單）開始練習，也可以選擇混合模式。

- LV1 到 LV5（從基礎到進階）
    
- 全部混合
    

### 2. 選擇玩法

你可以選擇這兩種練習方式：

- **看國字選注音**：練習正確的發音。
    
- **看注音選國字**：練習辨認正確的字。
    

### 3. 設定時間

你可以依照自己的速度練習：

- 不計時（沒有壓力）
    
- 限時 1 分鐘
    
- 限時 3 分鐘
    

## 練習結束後

遊戲結束後，網站會幫你整理出以下資訊：

- **你的成績**：告訴你答對了幾題，以及準確率。
    
- **錯題本**：把剛才答錯的字列出來，方便你再看一次。
    
- **最耗時的字**：列出你思考最久的字，讓你知道哪些字還不熟。
    

## 如何開始

1. 點開網址。
    
2. 照著網站上的步驟 1、2、3 點選你要的選項。
    
3. 按下「開始挑戰」按鈕。
    

網址：[https://mewomuguo.github.io/EasyHanziTW.io/]
